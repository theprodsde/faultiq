## Asking a Question

Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/theprodsde/faultiq/discussions) — use the **Q&A** category. Please include:

- What you're trying to do
- What you expected vs. what happened
- Relevant logs (`docker compose logs <service>`)
- Your OS + Docker version

## Reporting a Bug

Use the [bug report template](https://github.com/theprodsde/faultiq/issues/new?template=bug_report.md).

## Security Issues

Do **not** open a public issue. Follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
